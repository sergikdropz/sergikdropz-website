# OpenArt Create World: SERGIK Interdimensional Sound Laboratory

Reusable OpenArt "Describe Your World" definition for the SERGIK brand. The world is the persistent environment; the two scientist producers are separate OpenArt Characters cast into scenes, never baked into the world prompt. This keeps faces, clothes and tattoos consistent while the lab stays a stable, explorable location usable for cover art, visualizer backdrops and merch prints.

World name: **SERGIK Interdimensional Sound Laboratory** (in-world nickname: the Psychoacoustic Synth Lab).

---

## Paste-ready World prompt

```text
WORLD NAME: SERGIK Interdimensional Sound Laboratory (the Psychoacoustic Synth Lab)

A persistent psychedelic retro-futurist synthesizer laboratory and cosmic observatory hidden inside a surreal Sonoran desert dimension. A fully explorable environment, not a single composition.

IDENTITY: an eccentric underground analog synthesizer laboratory built by two unseen mad-scientist music producers. Part vintage 1970s electronic music studio, part experimental chemistry laboratory, part occult observatory, part alien research station, part Arizona desert hideout. Handcrafted and lived-in, never clean science fiction. No human figures appear in the world itself; people are added separately in Scene.

LANDMARKS, consistent from every camera angle:
- CENTER: a rugged wooden-and-metal chemistry workbench mounted on industrial caster wheels, topped by a large spherical glass flask of luminous swirling cosmic liquid, a miniature galaxy of pink, purple, cyan, blue, orange and yellow energy. A twisting column of psychedelic rainbow vapor rises from the flask toward the dome and slowly coils through the room. This flask and smoke are the visual anchor of the entire world.
- WEST WALL: a floor-to-ceiling Moog-inspired modular synthesizer wall, hundreds of colorful patch cables, patch bays, sequencers, oscilloscopes, VU meters, mixers, drum machines, keyboards, vacuum tube electronics; small screens showing animated waveforms, frequency spectrums and strange mathematical diagrams.
- EAST WALL: a second synthesizer wall of Korg-inspired, Roland-inspired and Akai-inspired machines, samplers and rack gear; several machines are partly organic, as though cables and strange life forms have slowly merged with the electronics.
- CEILING: a tall domed glass observatory ceiling framed by dark aged wooden beams. Through the glass: an extremely detailed deep-space sky, stars, colorful nebula clouds, distant galaxies, glowing moons, Saturn-like ringed planets, UFOs, shooting stars.
- WINDOWS: beyond them an impossible Sonoran desert, towering red rock mesas, scattered saguaro silhouettes, glowing alien plants, directly under the same starry sky, so desert and outer space share one horizon.
- NORTH: a copper spiral staircase climbing to a rooftop observatory platform with a brass telescope. Behind the synth walls: a small recording booth and a record-and-tape archive. Along one shelf: the alien specimen area, specimens in glass containers, small green extraterrestrial figures, growing crystals, human and animal skulls, mushrooms, cactus, occult geometry, third-eye imagery, eyes hidden in machinery. Through a half-open black metal door: a hidden portal chamber glowing with impossible light.

MATERIALS AND DETAILS: dark aged wood, copper, brass, black metal, exposed wiring, glass tubing; warm hanging industrial laboratory lamps, some containing surreal eye symbols; chemistry equipment everywhere, beakers, Erlenmeyer flasks, test tubes, spiral condensers, bubbling fluorescent liquids in radioactive neon green, electric cyan, ultraviolet purple, hot magenta, solar orange; transparent liquids with suspended particles like tiny stars; handwritten notebooks, occult diagrams, scorch marks, floating dust, objects that hover slightly off their surfaces. Evidence of hundreds of past experiments. The night sky seems to respond to the music.

PALETTE: electric cyan, ultraviolet purple, hot magenta, acid green, solar orange, golden yellow, deep cosmic blue. Saturated neons against deep blacks and warm wooden tones. Lighting combines warm orange laboratory lamps with colorful neon glow from chemicals, synthesizers, crystals, monitors and the cosmos.

STYLE: highly detailed hand-painted psychedelic cartoon illustration. Bold black ink outlines. Acrylic-painted texture. Screen-print and underground-comic influence. Retro 1970s science-fiction poster art. Slight punk and graffiti influence. Rich handmade imperfections. Strong foreground, middle-ground and background separation in every view.

AVOID: photorealism, sterile modern laboratories, generic futuristic spaceship interiors, human figures, blank empty walls, perfect symmetry.
```

## Shot menu (Scene prompts that reuse this world)

All shots reference the compass landmarks above so different generations read as the same universe.

1. Master establishing: wide shot from the south entrance down the main room, galaxy flask dead center, west and east synth walls in perspective, domed glass ceiling above, smoke column rising through frame.
2. Low angle: camera at floor level looking up past the caster-wheel workbench through the dome beams into the deep-space sky, smoke column crossing frame.
3. Overhead: top-down view of the chemistry workbench, bubbling experiments, notebooks, crystals, the galaxy flask as a bright center point.
4. Tracking shot: slow tracking left along the west modular wall toward the patch-bay corridor, patch cables and oscilloscope glow in foreground, dome edge in background.
5. Macro: close-up of the galaxy flask, swirling miniature nebula, condensation, synth LEDs reflected in the glass.
6. Interior peek: the small recording booth through its glass, tape archive shelves behind, one lamp with an eye symbol overhead.
7. Portal chamber: the half-open black metal door ajar, rainbow vapor bending toward the gap, faint impossible light spilling into the lab.
8. Rooftop platform: brass telescope silhouetted against the nebula sky, smoke column rising from a dome vent below, red mesas on the horizon.
9. Exterior: golden-hour desert view looking back at the laboratory carved into the rocks, dome glass glowing, smoke visible through the dome, saguaros in foreground.
10. Detail: alien specimen shelf close-up, specimens in jars, crystals growing beside a synth module, a small green figure hiding behind patch cables.

## Character notes (separate OpenArt Characters, cast in Scene)

Do not write the producers into the World prompt. Create each as a Character with a locked reference image, then Cast in Scene. Differentiation levers that map to the lab's own palette:

- Scientist A: one accent color family (cyan-leaning), one silhouette trait, one signature garment detail.
- Scientist B: the contrasting accent (magenta-leaning), contrasting build, contrasting glasses or headwear.
- Shared: lab coats, tattoos, analog accessories so they read as co-founders of the same lab.

## Continuity triggers (when a generation drifts)

- Shots disagree on ceiling: re-run with "domed glass observatory ceiling, never open air" moved into the first sentence.
- Flask wanders off-center: re-run with "the galaxy flask always sits at the center of the caster-wheel chemistry workbench."
- A character's face or outfit mutates: regenerate that Character's reference image; never add character details to the World prompt.
- Field truncates the prompt: cut the story section first, then the zone list; never cut IDENTITY, LANDMARKS, PALETTE or STYLE.
- Palette muddies: raise PALETTE above STYLE in importance by moving it before the STYLE block.
