# SERGIK Composition DNA

> Architectural framework for generating new compositions from SERGIK's body of work.
> Adapted from the Talon/Substrate architecture (MichaelCrowe11/talon) by mapping
> Substrate's long-form autobiographical composition grammar onto SERGIK's musical
> and psychological patterns — groove-forward underground electronic, dual-zone
> producer, Phoenix AZ, 2015-present.
>
> **The Substrate framework provides the architecture. SERGIK's DNA fills it.**

---

## 1. Source Basis

This DNA is derived from four source classes:

1. **Sonic DNA analysis** — AI-extracted attributes from 20,503 Ableton projects:
   BPM zones, key preferences (Camelot), energy profiles, genre distribution.
2. **Ableton project architecture** — stem structure from SERGIK's remix and production
   sets: MASTER REFERENCE, DRUMS (KICKS / CLAPS N SNARES / HATS / PERCUSSION),
   bass layers, melodic layers, FX/EFX chains.
3. **Finished exports** — 721 WAV masters on `/Volumes/SERGIK/Exports SERGIK/`
   as the canonical sonic reference for timbre, dynamics, and emotional character.
4. **Collaboration DNA** — 98 unique collaborators, top recurring: Silent Jay (soul vocals),
   Slick Floyd (electronic groove/funk), Breauxx (production complexity/bass),
   NOOD (bass experimentation), LODIN (melodic layers).

When sources disagree, use this precedence:
1. Composition canon = Sonic DNA profile + Ableton project architecture
2. Sonic DNA = finished WAV exports (timbre, loudness, dynamic behavior)
3. A generated composition only succeeds if it preserves both

## 2. The Core Contract

A composition is SERGIK DNA when it keeps all of the following intact:

1. **It is groove-forward** — the rhythm is the argument, not decoration.
2. **It moves through named sections** with deliberate dramatic function.
3. **It occupies a real environment** — Phoenix desert, warehouse concrete,
   after-hours space, or the internal geography of the studio.
4. **Its harmonic motion is chosen, not looped** — chords move with intent.
5. **It is reproducible stem by stem** from decomposed source material.
6. **It preserves SERGIK's dual-zone identity** — it is either grounded in
   the house/tech-house zone (120–128 BPM) or the downtempo/hip-hop zone (<90 BPM).
   Crossing zones is valid only if the transition is authored, not accidental.

## 3. Composition DNA

### 3.1 Section-first writing

SERGIK tracks are journeys, not loops. The section arc follows:

1. **Establish** — introduce the environment. Sparse. Patient. 30–90 seconds
   of world-building before the groove locks in.
2. **Argue** — introduce the groove thesis. First real statement of rhythm and harmony.
3. **Accumulate** — add density. Layers enter with purpose. Pressure builds.
4. **Break** — something releases, fractures, or transforms. Can be a drop,
   a filter sweep, a rhythmic interruption, or a harmonic pivot.
5. **Return** — the groove comes back changed. The listener has moved through something.

Tracks that skip the Break feel like extended loops. Tracks that skip the Return
feel unresolved. Both are violations.

### 3.2 Dual-zone pacing

**House/Tech House zone (120–128 BPM):**
- Intro stays minimal for 32–64 bars (≈ 60–120s at 126 BPM)
- Groove locks by bar 32 at the latest
- Climax earned by layer accumulation, not by a sudden drop
- Outro dissolves the groove rather than stopping it

**Downtempo/Hip-Hop zone (<90 BPM):**
- Intro allowed to float 60–120s with no strong pulse
- Swing and syncopation define the groove more than the kick pattern
- Climax may be textural rather than rhythmic
- Funk-memory patterns (broken beat, call-response) are primary

### 3.3 Harmonic identity

SERGIK's harmonic language favors major-key warmth with occasional dark pivots.

**Primary palette:**
- 10B (D major) — 31% — bright, energetic, forward motion
- 11B (A major) — 21% — warm, soulful, collaborative
- 7A (D minor) — 13% — dark, driving, hypnotic
- 8A (A minor) — 12% — melancholic, deep

**Chord language:**
- House zone: sustained 7ths and 9ths over moving bass, minimal chord rhythm
- Hip-hop zone: stacked samples, borrowed chords, unexpected major moments in minor contexts
- Funk zone: dominant 7ths, 13ths, tritone substitutions, II-V motion

**Harmonic thesis rule** (from Substrate): Every new composition must have one
declared harmonic thesis — a sentence describing what the harmony is doing and why.
"D major stays unresolved until the return section because the groove earns
resolution by accumulation, not by schedule" is a valid thesis.

### 3.4 Conversation pairs

Every strong SERGIK arrangement has at least one entangled voice pair — two layers
whose interaction creates meaning neither layer has alone.

Recurring pairs in SERGIK's work:
- kick and bass (body and drive)
- lead synth and pad (nerve and atmosphere)
- vocal chop and chord stab (human and machine)
- hat and percussion (grid and swing)
- guitar riff and organ (memory and motion)

The point: one voice acts as the groove's memory, the other acts as its momentum.

## 4. Environmental DNA

Every composition must have a room. The environment is not ornamental.
It tells the listener where the track exists before the first beat lands.

### SERGIK's six rooms

| Room | Description | Texture Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Desert heat** | Phoenix in summer — dry air, expansive silence, heat shimmer | filtered pink noise, distant wind, sparse reverb |
| **Warehouse concrete** | underground venue, 2am, sub pressure in the walls | sub drone, room resonance, crowd murmur at edges |
| **After-hours haze** | post-peak, 4am, fewer people, groove still holding | vinyl crackle, quieter room noise, fading crowd |
| **Studio dark** | SERGIK's production space, late night, creative isolation | low-level machine hum, click of keys, faint street |
| **Collaborative space** | session with Silent Jay or Slick Floyd — live creative exchange | breath, movement, voice fragments not yet committed to lyrics |
| **Internal geography** | psychological space — memory of a groove, anticipation of a drop | non-literal: reversed reverb tails, time-stretched texture |

The room must be:
- Audible in the intro (establishes the world)
- Audible in the outro (gives the last word back to the environment)
- Tied to the track's emotional subject, not chosen from a library

## 5. Arrangement DNA

### 5.1 Harmonic bed

The floor of the arrangement. Usually appears first. Often filters out
as layers enter, then re-emerges in the outro.

SERGIK's harmonic bed instruments:
- sustained pad (filtered, slow attack)
- organ chord (held, slight amp variation)
- piano chord (long tail, low-mid register)
- drone (bass note sustain, not rhythmic)

### 5.2 Emotional spear

The instrument that carries the groove's nerve — the one voice
that feels human even when everything around it is synthetic.

SERGIK's emotional spear candidates:
- wah guitar
- lead synth melody
- vocal chop (pitched/effected)
- acid bass line
- Rhodes or electric piano riff
- flute or brass stab

### 5.3 Pulse and gravity

Low-end motion that creates the sense of inevitability.
This is not just the kick drum — it is the reason the listener
stays in their body while listening.

SERGIK's low-end architecture (from Ableton sets):
- KICKS (main kick, layered for punch and sub)
- CLAPS N SNARES (backbeat identity)
- HATS (swing definition)
- PERCUSSION (additional syncopation)
- bass line (melodic or static root motion)
- sub layer (felt, not heard)

### 5.4 Orchestral bloom

The layers that enter in the accumulation section and fill the arrangement.

SERGIK's bloom palette:
- string sample or pad swell
- additional percussion layer
- secondary melodic voice (collaboration track)
- chopped vocal swell (from Silent Jay collaborations)
- wider reverb on existing layers (automated send increase)

## 6. Render DNA

### 6.1 Stem-first discipline

Every generated composition is built stem by stem.
No single-pass renders. No "export the session mix."

The stem order reflects dramatic importance:
1. Environment/texture (first layer — sets the room)
2. Kick (defines the zone — house or downtempo)
3. Bass (harmonic root and groove anchor)
4. Harmonic bed (emotional floor)
5. Emotional spear (the nerve)
6. Percussion layers (swing identity)
7. Additional melodic layers (accumulation)
8. Bloom elements (climax)
9. FX/EFX tails (transitions)
10. Outro decay (environmental layer returns)

### 6.2 Per-stem processing chain

Each stem gets its own post chain before compositing. Based on
SERGIK's Ableton architecture (EFX track, Dry/Wet routing):

| Stem role | Chain |
|---|---|
| Kick | highpass 30Hz, equalizer (punch 80Hz, cut 300Hz), acompressor (fast attack), limiter |
| Bass | highpass 40Hz, equalizer (body 200Hz, cut 2kHz), acompressor, sidechain from kick |
| Pad/bed | lowpass 8kHz, aecho (long tail, room size), afade in/out, volume |
| Lead/spear | highpass 200Hz, equalizer (presence 2–4kHz), aecho (medium), volume |
| Percussion | equalizer, aecho (short), acompressor, volume |
| Environment | lowpass 800Hz OR bandpass, volume automation (intro/outro), no compression |
| Vocal chop | highpass 120Hz, equalizer (2kHz lift), acompressor, afade, ducking from kick |

### 6.3 Mastering targets

| Parameter | SERGIK standard | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Target LUFS | -12 (±2) | Louder than Substrate's -14; dance floor optimized |
| True Peak | -1.0 dBTP | Standard for streaming |
| Dynamic Range | >6 dB | Groove tracks, not ambient; allow density |
| Stereo Correlation | >0.7 | Mono-compatible for club PA systems |

## 8. Emotional DNA

SERGIK's compositions do not chase generic moods. Each centers one lived state
and develops it. Recurring emotional materials derived from catalog analysis:

- **patient expansion** — the groove earns its fullness; nothing is given early
- **underground solidarity** — the dance floor as shared experience, not spectacle
- **funk inheritance** — the soul and hip-hop lineage felt beneath the electronic surface
- **desert solitude** — Phoenix geography: heat, space, distance from the center
- **collaborative warmth** — the specific texture of making something with people who trust you
- **after-hours truth** — what remains when the room empties; the groove that stays honest

## 9. What Future Compositions Must Preserve

A generated composition is valid SERGIK DNA when:

1. The section arc is written before rendering starts
2. The harmonic thesis is a complete sentence
3. The environment is real and subject-specific
4. Stems are rendered and shaped independently
5. The groove earns its resolution — it does not snap to a climax on schedule
6. The outro carries the room back in — the environment gets the last word
7. It is mastered to -12 LUFS / -1 dBTP with >6 dB dynamic range

## 10. Decomposition → Composition Pipeline

### Step 1: Source decomposition
For each source WAV from `/Volumes/SERGIK/Exports SERGIK/`:
- **Stem separation**: Demucs v4 (drums, bass, other, vocals)
- **MIDI extraction**: Basic Pitch (melody → MIDI, bass → MIDI)
- **Spectrogram analysis**: ffmpeg spectrogram filter + librosa analysis
  - dominant frequencies per section
  - energy curve over time
  - key estimation from chromagram

### Step 2: DNA manifest generation
From analysis results, auto-populate a `sergik-dna-manifest.json` per track:
- Detected BPM → zone assignment
- Detected key → Camelot wheel position
- Energy curve → section boundary detection
- Spectrogram peaks → emotional arc candidates
- Stem shapes → arrangement role assignments

### Step 3: Composition synthesis
Using the manifest as a score, generate new MIDI arrangements:
- Root key: use SERGIK's detected primary key or a Camelot-compatible neighbor
- Section durations: derived from source section analysis
- Harmonic motion: constrained to SERGIK's chord language (7ths, 9ths, funk dominants)
- Groove pattern: extracted from Demucs drum stem → quantized MIDI

### Step 4: Stem render + post chain
- Each generated MIDI stem rendered through appropriate synthesis
- Per-stem processing chain applied (see Section 6.2)
- Stems composited with authored ducking and timing

### Step 5: Validation
Run `validate_sergik_manifest.py` to confirm:
- Section map written ✓
- Harmonic thesis present ✓
- Environmental texture present ✓
- Stem isolation retained ✓
- Mastering targets met ✓
