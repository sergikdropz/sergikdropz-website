## Cross-EP Pattern Analysis

### Key Distribution
- **D major (10B)**: 5 EPs — Daze, In The Streets, Soul Candy, Utopia (+ Inspire adjacent)
- **G# major (4B)**: 2 EPs — Staying A Vibe, Vice & Virtues (paired EPs)
- **C major (8B)**: 1 EP — Are We Awake
- **A major (11B)**: 1 EP — Inspire
- **G major (9B)**: 1 EP — The World Dont Stop
- **E minor (9A)**: 1 EP — FTP (the only minor-key primary EP)

**SERGIK DNA confirmation**: D major (10B) dominates as predicted — 5 of 10 EPs.

### Zone Distribution
- **Tech House**: 5 EPs (Are We Awake, Staying A Vibe, The World Dont Stop, Utopia, Vice & Virtues)
- **Funk Fusion**: 3 EPs (Daze, FTP, Inspire)
- **House**: 2 EPs (In The Streets, Soul Candy)
- **No pure downtempo EP** — lowest avg BPM is Inspire at 102

### Stem Group Prominences (cross-EP avg)
- **BASS**: consistently highest prominence (sub + bass range dominant)
- **DRUMS**: high prominence (transient energy)
- **MELODIC**: moderate (250Hz–4kHz content)
- **ATMOSPHERE**: lowest (high-frequency texture)

This matches SERGIK's DNA: **pulse and gravity first**, melodic content second.
